Most hospitality and short-stay businesses aren't short on effort. Rooms get filled, guests arrive, teams stay busy. But revenue, distribution and operations are rarely working together — so activity doesn't translate into commercial performance.

Pat & Care exists to close that gap. We connect revenue strategy, OTA and distribution, commercial planning and day-to-day operations into one coherent view of what's actually driving — or holding back — your property's performance.

We're not a booking manager or a staffing agency. We're a commercial and performance partner for hospitality operators who want to know exactly what's working, what isn't, and what to do about it.
